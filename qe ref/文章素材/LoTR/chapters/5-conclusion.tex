\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
\vspace{-1mm}

We presented LoTR, a plug-and-play reasoning controller that strengthens existing reasoning paradigms by routing each step through an internal pathway matched to its inferred logic state. The key insight is that reasoning quality depends not only on the external scaffold a model follows, but also on the internal computation through which each step is realized. LoTR implements this view by extracting a shared logic-state basis, reading state activations with lightweight probes, and composing reusable head-level routing templates at inference time. Across models, benchmarks, and paradigms, LoTR improves the accuracy--efficiency frontier under controlled cost, showing that internal pathway control can serve as a practical complement to external reasoning design. These results point to a broader direction: improving LLM reasoning requires not only better ways to organize thoughts externally, but also better pathways for carrying them out internally.
