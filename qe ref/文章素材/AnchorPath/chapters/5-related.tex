\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{One-shot training-free structured pruning.}
LLM-Pruner~\citep{ma2023llmpruner}, FLAP~\citep{an2024flap}, Wanda~\citep{sun2024wanda}, and SliceGPT~\citep{ashkboos2024slicegpt} build a single importance model at the dense point and extrapolate it across the whole budget, then typically restore accuracy with LoRA or fine-tuning.
They are the $K{=}1$ case of \method, compared \emph{with recovery disabled} to isolate selection; their shared high-sparsity collapse (Sec.~\ref{sec:collapse}) is the phenomenon we address.

\textbf{Closest neighbor: the LLM Surgeon.}
\citet{ouderaa2024llmsurgeon} re-estimates Kronecker-factored Fisher curvature at the current pruned model over many shots, and even observes that the first-order term does not vanish along the path.
Our distinction is one of \emph{regime}: the Surgeon interleaves Optimal-Brain-Surgeon~\citep{hassibi1993obs} weight updates and LoRA, so anchor and expansion point move together, whereas \method updates \emph{no} weights and folds the resulting drift term into \emph{selection}.

\textbf{Reconstruction-based second-order pruning.}
A large line reconstructs weights under (inverse-)Hessian objectives: OBC~\citep{frantar2022obc}, SparseGPT~\citep{frantar2023sparsegpt}, ZipLM~\citep{kurtic2023ziplm}, WoodFisher~\citep{singh2020woodfisher}, and the structured SlimGPT~\citep{ling2024slimgpt}/SlimLLM~\citep{guo2025slimllm}.
\method updates neither weights nor a global inverse Hessian; benchmarked on selection alone, the structured members collapse below even one-shot, so their reported quality is the value of reconstruction, not selection.

\textbf{Iterative and search-based pruning.}
A concurrent line reaches gradualness by global \emph{search} or \emph{weight adjustment}: T\'yr-the-Pruner~\citep{li2025tyr}, evolutionary per-layer search (DarwinLM~\citep{tang2025darwinlm}, EvoPress~\citep{sieberling2025evopress}), staged or joint-granularity removal (2SSP~\citep{sandri2025twossp}, AMP~\citep{mugnaini2025amp} after SAViT~\citep{zheng2022savit}), depth pruners (SLEB~\citep{song2024sleb}, BlockPruner~\citep{zhong2025blockpruner}), and probe-based inference-time pruning~\citep{gong2026subspacepath}.
None re-centers a curvature model against a \emph{fixed} anchor while moving only the expansion point.

\textbf{Roots and positioning.}
Budget-indexed continuation (SpODE~\citep{mo2023spode}), the $1/K^2$ bound, and first-order Taylor importance~\citep{molchanov2019taylor,lee2019snip} are each classical in isolation; our contribution is their \emph{combination} in the calibration-only, weight-frozen regime, rooted in Optimal Brain Damage/Surgeon~\citep{lecun1989obd,hassibi1993obs}, Fisher pruning~\citep{theis2018faster}, and the Combinatorial Brain Surgeon~\citep{yu2022cbs}.
Prune-then-distill methods (Sheared-LLaMA~\citep{xia2024sheared}, Minitron~\citep{muralidharan2024minitron}) instead let retraining do the work; our masks compose with any such recovery.
