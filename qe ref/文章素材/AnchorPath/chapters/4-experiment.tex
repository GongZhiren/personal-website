\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:exp}

\subsection{Setup}
\textbf{Models.}
We evaluate seven open dense decoder-only LLMs spanning five families, three scales, and distinct tokenizers and attention designs: LLaMA-2-7B~\citep{touvron2023llama2}, LLaMA-3.1-8B and LLaMA-3.2-3B~\citep{dubey2024llama3}, Qwen2.5-7B and Qwen2.5-14B~\citep{qwen2025qwen25} (GQA), Yi-1.5-9B~\citep{ai2024yi}, and Falcon3-7B~\citep{falcon3}.
LLaMA-3.1-8B is the lead model (Table~\ref{tab:main}); the canonical LLaMA-2-7B carries the mechanistic analysis; the rest test cross-family generalization.

\textbf{Datasets and metrics.}
We report a broad suite: perplexity on WikiText-2~\citep{merity2017wikitext}, PTB~\citep{marcus1993ptb}, and C4~\citep{raffel2020t5}; zero-shot accuracy on seven commonsense tasks~\citep{clark2018arc,zellers2019hellaswag,sakaguchi2021winogrande,bisk2020piqa,mihaylov2018openbookqa,clark2019boolq} and MMLU~\citep{hendrycks2021mmlu}; and generation~\citep{cobbe2021gsm8k,chen2021humaneval,austin2021mbpp}. The recovery-free teacher KL is logged per step.

\textbf{Baselines (selection-only, matched sparsity, no weight modification).}
We compare against one-shot second-order pruning (the $K{=}1$ ablation of \method), Wanda-sp~\citep{sun2024wanda}, FLAP~\citep{an2024flap}, LLM-Pruner~\citep{ma2023llmpruner} with LoRA disabled, magnitude, and random --- all \emph{selection criteria}, so the comparison isolates \emph{which} units to remove, exactly \method's regime.
Every method shares the calibration set, protected-layer band, and per-layer cap, and all post-selection adjustments (FLAP's bias compensation, LLM-Pruner's LoRA) are disabled uniformly.
Reconstruction-based pruners (SlimGPT~\citep{ling2024slimgpt}, SlimLLM~\citep{guo2025slimllm}) add a weight-reconstruction stage; run selection-only under this protocol they too collapse below one-shot at high sparsity, so their reported quality measures reconstruction, which composes with a better mask.

\subsection{Main Results}
\label{sec:mainres}
The headline is a change in \emph{curve shape} (Fig.~\ref{fig:headline}, Table~\ref{tab:main}). On
LLaMA-3.1-8B one-shot is competitive at low sparsity but collapses super-exponentially (WikiText ppl
$246$/$4106$ at $\rho{=}0.4$/$0.5$), as does \emph{every} training-free baseline: Wanda-sp and FLAP
exceed $10^3$ by $\rho{=}0.3$--$0.4$, and even the strongest, gradient LLM-Pruner ($718$ at $\rho{=}0.5$),
trails widely, consistent with the paradigm diagnosis of Sec.~\ref{sec:collapse}. \method bends the curve,
forming the lower envelope at every ratio ($\mathbf{5.2\times}$/$\mathbf{16.8\times}$ below one-shot at
$\rho{=}0.4$/$0.5$), and the same shape change holds across all seven models (Table~\ref{tab:crossmodel}).
The margins are not a single-draw artifact: a three-seed resample gives $35.6{\pm}1.5$ at $\rho{=}0.4$ on
LLaMA-2-7B ($\pm4\%$), far inside the reported gaps.

\label{sec:crossmodel}
\textbf{The universal default generalizes across families} (Table~\ref{tab:crossmodel}). Stripped to its
conservative form (\emph{equal-cost} bands, no per-model tuning), the $K$-step continuation is below
one-shot on \emph{every} model at \emph{every} ratio, and at $\rho{=}0.5$ is the lower envelope of the
full training-free set on all seven models.
The gain is largest where one-shot collapses hardest: $\mathbf{16.8\times}$ on LLaMA-3.1-8B and $\mathbf{9.3\times}$ on LLaMA-3.2-3B at $\rho{=}0.5$, versus $1.9\times$ on the mildly collapsing Qwen2.5-7B.
Yi-1.5-9B is the extreme case: every activation-, weight-, and one-shot-second-order method exceeds $10^4$ at $\rho{=}0.5$ (a model property, not our baseline), whereas the continuation holds it at $61.7$ and its multiplier grows to $\mathbf{119\times}$ at $\rho{=}0.6$.
It also matches or beats gradient LLM-Pruner, the strongest selection-only baseline, at every cell (e.g.\ $245$ vs.\ $718$ on LLaMA-3.1-8B).

\textbf{The gain is not confined to perplexity.} The equal-band default also lifts the downstream zero-shot average above both one-shot and the strongest no-recovery baseline (LLM-Pruner) at $\rho{\ge}0.4$ on LLaMA-3.1-8B, Yi-1.5-9B, and Qwen2.5-7B, and on LLaMA-2-7B at $\rho{=}0.5$; the one moderate-sparsity task it trades away (BoolQ at $\rho{=}0.3$) is recovered \emph{above} one-shot by the optional schedule refinement (Sec.~\ref{sec:whitebox}).

\subsection{Ablations}
\label{sec:ablations}
Table~\ref{tab:ablation} reads as a \emph{build-up} on LLaMA-2-7B at $\rho{=}0.5$: one-shot ($1497$) $\to$ multi-step re-estimation ($K{=}8$, equal bands; $388.7$, the $3.9\times$ default), which an optional schedule refinement compounds to $125.6$ ($11.9\times$).
The toggles then isolate each factor.
\textbf{Refresh $\tau{=}0.5$ is a robust interior default}: it ($388.7$) beats both a full refresh ($\tau{=}1.0$, $583$, which adds variance from noisily estimated weakly coupled units) and a thin refresh that leaves curvature stale, so we read $\tau$ as variance control rather than ``more is better''.
\textbf{The fixed dense anchor} defines the risk as KL to the dense teacher rather than the drifting operating point, and yields the global-best configuration; \textbf{removing the revived first-order term} regresses $\rho{=}0.5$ from $388.7$ to $667.7$.

\paragraph{Robustness.} One fixed configuration ($K{=}8$, $\tau{=}0.5$, \emph{equal-cost} bands, shared caps) serves \emph{all seven} models with no per-model tuning, and the calibration set is held identical for every method, so the comparison isolates selection.
The safety rails are not the source of the gain: removing both the per-layer cap and the protected band \emph{widens} the advantage over one-shot to $18.1\times$.

\subsection{Deployment Efficiency}
\label{sec:efficiency}
Because \method removes whole heads and FFN groups, the pruned model is \emph{physically exported} to a
smaller dense network. At a matched ratio it and one-shot export to the same budget, so \method is a
\emph{pure quality gain at zero deployment overhead} --- identical parameters, FLOPs, and latency --- on
top of the export's \good{$1.6\times$ prefill speedup} and \good{$36\%$ lower peak memory} over dense at
$\rho{=}0.5$ (LLaMA-2-7B). The one-time \emph{offline} cost is $\approx(1{+}(K{-}1)\tau)\times$ one shot
($4.5\times$ default), negligible beside fine-tuning.
